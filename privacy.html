Awake privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026

Awake is an alarm clock app for Android. This policy explains what Awake does with your information. The short version: Awake keeps your alarms and progress on your phone, shows no ads, has no analytics or tracking, and sells nothing about you.

What stays on your phone

  • Your alarms, settings, wake tasks, points, achievements, and history.
  • Your sleep log, bedtime, and sleep sound choices.
  • The bathroom code or NFC tag you save, the places you save for Destination Check-in, and any task calibration.
  • Sound files you choose for an alarm (Awake keeps its own copy on the phone).

Camera frames, NFC tag reads, light levels, motion readings, and location checks are processed on the phone while a task is open and thrown away right after. Awake keeps only the result, such as "scanned" or "arrived". None of this is uploaded.

What leaves your phone

  • Morning weather (off unless you turn it on). To show today's forecast when your alarm turns off, Awake sends your rough location (rounded to about 1 km) to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway), which provides the forecast. See MET Norway's privacy policy.
  • Feedback you send. If you use Send feedback, your message, the Awake version, and your phone's model and Android version go to Formspree, a form service that forwards them to the developer by email. Include contact details only if you want a reply.
  • Purchases. Awake+ is a subscription sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; Awake never sees your card or payment details, only whether your account has Awake+.
  • Ratings. If you rate Awake, Google Play's own review window handles it.

Permissions Awake asks for

  • Alarms and reminders, notifications, and full-screen alarms: to ring at the exact time you set and open your wake task over the lock screen.
  • Camera: only while you scan the code you saved for Bathroom Scan or a check-in.
  • NFC: only to read the ID of the tag you saved.
  • Location: only when you check in at a place you saved (checked on the phone), and for Morning weather if you turn it on.
  • Run at startup and keep sound playing: to set your alarms again after the phone restarts, and to keep the alarm and sleep sounds playing.
  • Vibration: for alarms and haptic feedback.

You can turn any permission off in your phone's settings. Awake tells you what stops working and offers another way where it can.

What Awake does not do

  • No ads, no advertising IDs, and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • No selling or renting your information.
  • No accounts in this version: you don't sign in, and nothing about you is stored on a server.

Children

Awake is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

Keeping and deleting your information

Everything Awake keeps is on your phone. You can export it from Settings › Privacy & permissions › Export my data, and deleting the app deletes it. To have a feedback message you sent deleted, ask through Send feedback.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. When Awake adds accounts or anything else that changes what is collected, this page will say so before that version is released.

Contact

Use Send feedback in Awake (Settings › Help & feedback), or the developer email on Awake's Google Play page.
