Delete your Awake account

Awake, the alarm app, by its developer. Updated October 17, 2026.

In the app

  1. Open Awake and go to Settings › Account.
  2. Tap Delete account, then hold the button to confirm.

Your account and everything stored for it are deleted right away. Your alarms keep working on that phone as a guest.

Without the app

Send a request with the email of the Google account you signed in to Awake with. The account and its data are deleted within 30 days, and you get a reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in: the Google account email and name Awake's sign-in service (Google Firebase) keeps.
  • Your leaderboard row: your display name and point totals.
  • Your backup: the alarms, settings, points, morning history, and sleep log backed up to your account.

Nothing is kept after deletion. What Awake stores on your phone stays there until you delete the app.

Never signed in?

Then the only thing on Awake's servers is your leaderboard row, if you're on the leaderboard. Turn off Show me on the leaderboard in Settings › Progress & leaderboard to delete it, or delete the app.
